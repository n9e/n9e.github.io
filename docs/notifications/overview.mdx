Six media types ship configured and enabled — FlashDuty, Callback, Email, DingTalk, WeCom and
Feishu Card — and 20 built-in message templates cover the rest: Slack, Telegram, Discord, Mattermost,
PagerDuty, FlashDuty, Jira and webhooks. Each message is rendered from a template; the variables
available to those templates are listed in the [reference](../reference/notification-variables.md).
